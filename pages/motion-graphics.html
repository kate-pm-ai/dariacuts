Motion Graphics That Make Your Message Clearer

Motion can show a relationship, introduce a section or direct attention to the part of the screen that matters. I use timing, scale, typography and transitions to make information easier to follow while keeping the visual treatment connected to the rest of the video.

The aim is not movement for its own sake. Each animated element needs a job: carry a line of text, reveal a product feature, connect scenes or give supplied brand material a consistent rhythm.

The approach

Motion Graphics for Existing Videos or Standalone Assets

Animated typeBrand elementsVideo integration

Motion graphics can be developed inside a wider edit or supplied as an agreed standalone element. For an existing video, I work with the cut, timing and available brand material so titles, callouts or graphic transitions feel integrated rather than added afterwards.

For a separate animated asset, send the intended placement, duration, dimensions, brand guide, graphics and reference material. We define how the animation will be used before I establish the design and movement.

Before we start

Motion Graphics FAQs

What are motion graphics services?

Motion graphics services cover animated typography, graphic elements, interfaces, infographics and branded visuals for video. They focus on moving graphic information and differ from full 3D production or character-led animation.

How much does motion graphics design cost?

The quote depends on the number of scenes, design complexity, duration, asset preparation and agreed review scope. Send me the intended use and references so I can understand the work before confirming a price.

Can you add motion graphics to an existing video?

Yes. I can create agreed titles, transitions, callouts or graphic sequences within an existing edit. Please share the current video, available brand assets and examples of the visual direction you want to explore.

Can you animate a logo or brand identity?

I can assess supplied logo artwork and brand elements for an agreed animation task. Send the available files, brand guidance and intended placement so I can confirm what is practical within the project scope.

What files do you need to start?

Please share a short brief, visual references, the intended dimensions and any existing brand guide, fonts, graphics, video or interface assets. I will confirm which source files are suitable before work begins.

What’s the difference between motion graphics and 2D animation?

Motion graphics usually centre on type, interfaces, diagrams and graphic information. My 2D animation work uses more illustrative, collage and frame-by-frame techniques. The two can overlap within one video when the brief calls for both.
