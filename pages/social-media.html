What I edit

Social Media Videos I Can Edit

Short Vertical Clips

Short clips built for vertical viewing, with a clear opening and a steady pace. I select the strongest moments from your footage and shape them into compact videos that are easy to follow on a small screen. The opening is built to make sense in the first second.

Brand & Product Videos

Videos that present a product, a service or a brand moment. I organise the footage into a clear sequence and use your colours, fonts and supplied assets to keep the look consistent from one video to the next.

Cutdowns from Longer Videos

Interviews, podcasts, YouTube videos and other long recordings can supply shorter social videos. I choose self-contained moments and rebuild them into concise edits with a clear beginning and end. Each one can stand on its own in the feed.

Captions, Titles & Motion Details

Captions, titles and restrained motion details help viewers follow a video without sound. As part of my social media video editing services, I add them in the agreed style, so they support the message rather than crowd the frame.

Before we start

Social Media Video Editing FAQs

What affects the price of social media video editing?

The price depends on the number of videos, the amount of raw footage, the versions you need, text and graphics, and the scope of revisions. Send your brief and a sample, and I will confirm the price.

Which types of social media videos can you edit?

I edit brand videos, product videos, informational clips and short vertical videos from your footage. Tell me the format you have in mind, and I will confirm whether it fits the project.

Can one long recording be turned into videos for several platforms?

Yes, when this is agreed in the scope. We choose the fragments, their length and aspect ratios together, and I export a separate file for each platform you plan to use.

What should I provide before the editing starts?

Send the footage, references, your visual style or brand guide, the required sizes, any text for titles and captions, and your deadline. I will confirm the file-sharing method before we start.

Can you add captions and branded motion graphics?

Yes, within the agreed scope. I can add captions, titles and branded motion details in the style of your brand guide. More complex animation can be discussed as a separate motion graphics project.

How do you handle revisions and final video formats?

We agree the number of revisions and the final formats when we discuss the project. You send your comments together after reviewing the edit, and I export the approved files in the agreed formats.
