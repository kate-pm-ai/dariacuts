What I do

Video Editing & Animation Services

I provide professional video editing services across digital, social and story-led formats. Each project is shaped around the brief, the audience and the material you already have.

YouTube Video Editing

I shape long-form footage into a focused episode with considered pacing, clear structure and visual details that support the story without distracting from it.

Short-Form Video Editing (Reels / Shorts / TikTok)

Podcast Video Editing

I edit multi-part conversations and can handle colour correction, visual clean-up and distracting-object removal when the footage needs a more polished finish.

Brand & Corporate Video Editing

I create clean, purposeful brand and corporate edits that organise the message, maintain visual consistency and make complex material easier to follow.

Documentary & Trailer Editing

I build documentary pieces and trailers around character, atmosphere and narrative progression, selecting moments that carry the emotional direction of the film.

2D Animation

I design and animate 2D elements that explain an idea, add personality or connect live-action footage with a distinct visual language.

I combine cut-out imagery, layered compositions, frame-by-frame movement and compositing for tactile, playful visuals with a distinctly handmade visual character.

Story first

Editing That Brings Your Story Together

Good editing is not simply a series of clean cuts. I look for the thread that holds the material together: what the viewer needs to understand, where the pace should change and which details deserve space.

From a YouTube episode to a compact brand film, I use structure, sound, colour and motion to create one coherent experience. The result is an edit that feels intentional and keeps the story moving without losing its personality.

Before we start

Video Editing FAQs

How much do video editing services cost?

I assess the format, footage volume, animation, revision scope and final deliverables before quoting. Tell me about your idea in a message; we can clarify the details and references together before I confirm a price.

What types of videos do you edit?

I edit YouTube episodes, short-form social videos, podcasts, brand and corporate content, documentaries and trailers. The selected work above includes a comedy vlog, Gartner video and podcast episode, alongside my animation projects.

Can I work with you remotely from the UK?

Yes. I am based in Barcelona and work remotely with UK and international clients. Before starting, I confirm availability, the required formats and how we will share footage, review the edit and deliver files.

How do I get started with a video editing project?

If you want to hire a video editor, start with a WhatsApp message about your idea. You do not need a finished brief: I’ll ask about the audience, footage and references, then we’ll agree the next steps.

Can you add 2D animation or motion graphics?

Yes. I create animated titles, kinetic typography, graphic transitions and collage sequences where they support the brief. You can see examples in my Kinetic Typography Video and Collage Animation projects in the portfolio above.

How do revisions work?

I agree the review process and revision scope with you before starting. You share consolidated feedback on the cut, and I apply the agreed changes. Any additional requests need to be discussed against that scope.
