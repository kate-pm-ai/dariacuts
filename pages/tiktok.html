Before we start

TikTok Video Editing FAQs

What makes a TikTok video engaging from the first few seconds?

A clear hook, an action the viewer understands immediately, readable captions and a quick pace. I build the opening around these elements, but no edit can guarantee that a video will be recommended.

Can you create TikTok clips from longer videos?

Yes. Interviews, vlogs and other long recordings can supply short clips. I select self-contained moments and edit them for vertical viewing; the exact number and formats of clips are agreed in advance.

Can you add captions and on-screen text to TikTok videos?

Yes. I add captions and text based on the script or audio you provide, in the agreed style. We confirm the language and how the subtitles will be checked before editing starts.

What affects the price of TikTok video editing?

The price depends on the length of the raw footage, the number of clips, the complexity of graphics, any subtitles or extra versions and the revisions needed. Send your brief, and I will confirm the price.

Can you edit TikTok videos recorded on a phone?

Yes. Phone footage is a common starting point. I review the quality, sound and format first and tell you what can be improved, although not every recording issue can be fixed in the edit.

Do you publish TikTok videos or only provide the edited files?

I provide the edited files, ready for you to upload. Posting, account management and content strategy are not included by default, as my work focuses on the video editing itself.
