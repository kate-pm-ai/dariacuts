Every second matters

Make Every Second of Your Short Video Count

A short video has very little time to establish context, hold attention and deliver its point. I look for the strongest opening, remove pauses that slow the idea down and build a rhythm that feels purposeful without making the edit frantic.

The work can also include vertical reframing, captions, on-screen text and motion details that keep the content readable on a phone, including when the sound is off. The result is a clear, compact edit that still sounds and feels like the creator or brand behind it.

The edit

From Raw Footage to a Ready-to-Post Clip

Hook & pacingVertical framingCaptions & graphics

I start by finding the point of the clip and the material that communicates it most clearly. I remove unnecessary pauses, organise the strongest moments and shape the pace around the intended tone.

The agreed short video editing service can also cover vertical reframing, captions, graphic details, sound balancing and exports in the dimensions required for the selected platforms, creating a clean and consistent viewing experience. Uploading, channel management and content strategy are separate from the edit unless they are specifically agreed as part of a project.

Before we start

Short-Form Video Editing FAQs

What platforms do you edit short-form videos for?

I edit vertical videos for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. We agree the intended platforms and required versions before work begins. My role covers the edit and prepared files rather than uploading or managing your accounts.

Can you edit short clips from long-form footage?

Yes. I can review longer footage and select moments that work as self-contained clips when repurposing is part of the brief. We agree the themes, number of clips and intended formats before editing starts.

Do you add captions to short videos?

Yes, captions or other on-screen text can be included in the agreed edit. Please confirm the language, wording and any brand requirements so the text can be prepared and checked before the final export.

How much does short-form video editing cost?

The quote depends on the amount of footage, number of videos, graphic treatment and formats required. Send me your clips and references so I can understand the work before confirming a price.

Can you match my brand’s style?

I can work from an existing brand guide, supplied fonts, colours and visual references. We will clarify which assets are available and agree the treatment before I apply it across the requested videos.

How many revisions do I get?

The review process and number of agreed revision rounds are confirmed before the project starts. This keeps the scope clear and gives us a shared point of reference when you send feedback.
