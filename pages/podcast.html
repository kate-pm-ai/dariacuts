What Podcast Video Editing Can Include

Multi-Camera Conversation Cuts

When an episode is recorded on more than one camera, I can sync the angles and cut between speakers so reactions and answers land naturally. We agree this as part of the scope once I have seen your files.

Pauses, Mistakes & Pacing

I remove long silences, repeated phrases and interruptions that break the flow, while keeping natural reactions. The aim is a conversation that moves without sounding rushed or over-cut.

Titles, Name Tags & Visual Inserts

Guest names, topic titles and simple visual inserts help viewers follow who is speaking and what the discussion is about. The style follows your brief and any existing branding.

Episode Highlights & Short Clips

A full episode can also supply short clips for promotion. When agreed separately, I select the strongest moments and export them in the formats your channels need.

Podcast post production follows four clear stages, so you always know what happens with your recording next.

Episode Structure

Before we start

Podcast Video Editing FAQs

What is included in professional video podcast editing?

It usually covers assembling the episode, trimming pauses and mistakes, switching camera angles and adding titles or inserts from the brief. The amount of audio work depends on your recording and is confirmed before editing starts.

Can you edit footage recorded with more than one camera?

Multi-camera footage can be part of the project. Share the separate camera files, the audio source and an example of the cutting style you like, and I will confirm how the edit will work.

Can a full podcast episode be turned into short social clips?

Yes, when agreed as a separate part of the project. I select meaningful moments, cut them into self-contained clips and export them for the platforms you plan to use.

How much does video podcast editing cost?

The price depends on episode length, amount of footage, number of cameras, graphics or subtitles and any clips you need. Send your brief and a sample recording, and I will confirm the price.

What do I need to send before you can edit an episode?

Send the video and audio files, camera order, references, your logo, required formats, timecodes and notes on what to keep or remove. I will confirm the file-sharing method before we start.

How are revisions handled for podcast video editing?

We agree the review process and timing before editing begins. You review the edit and send your comments together, then I make the agreed changes.
